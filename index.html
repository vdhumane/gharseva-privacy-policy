GharSeva — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Welcome to GharSeva ("we", "our", or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who We Are

GharSeva is a local home services marketplace that connects customers with service providers (plumbers, electricians, maids, and home tiffin providers) in their area. The app is developed and operated by Vikrant Dhumane.

2. Information We Collect

a) Account Information

  • Customer accounts: Name, email address, phone number, society name, flat number, and password (stored in encrypted form)
  • Vendor accounts: Name, email address, phone number, service category, description, address, operating areas, Aadhaar verification status, profile photo, business photos, and GPS location

b) Service Request Information

  • Service requests you send or receive
  • Problem descriptions
  • Preferred service times
  • Society name and flat number (for service delivery)
  • Request status and responses

c) Location Information

  • We collect your approximate location (GPS coordinates) to show you nearby service providers
  • Vendors' business locations are used to calculate distance from customers

d) Photos

  • Profile photos (optional for customers, mandatory for vendors)
  • Business photos (optional, up to 3 images for vendors)

e) Device Information

  • Device push notification token (FCM token) to send you important notifications about your service requests

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To connect customers with service providers in their area
  • To send service requests and receive responses
  • To send push notifications about request status updates
  • To verify vendor identities (Aadhaar verification status display)
  • To improve our app and services

4. How We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • With service providers: When you send a service request, the vendor sees your name, phone number, society name, and flat number so they can provide the service
  • With customers: Vendors' public profiles (name, category, photos, services, charges) are visible to customers
  • With service providers (infrastructure): We use Supabase (database and authentication) and Firebase (push notifications) to operate our services

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers
  • Passwords are stored in encrypted form
  • We use secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission
  • Photos are stored as encoded data in our database

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information (you can update your profile in the app)
  • Delete your account and personal information (contact us to request deletion)
  • Withdraw consent for location sharing (you can deny location permission)
  • Opt out of push notifications (you can disable them in your device settings)

7. Children's Privacy

GharSeva is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal information within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice in the app or via other communication methods.

10. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notifications
  • Google Play Services: Location services

These services have their own privacy policies which can be found on their respective websites.
